Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

1. Overview

Burion Studio operates this website as an independent game and software studio. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the website.

2. Information you provide

If you contact Burion Studio through the website, information you enter in the contact form may include your name, email address, subject and message. The current form opens your email client using a mailto link; the website itself does not send the message to a server.

3. Language preference

The website stores your selected language in your browser's localStorage. This preference is used only to remember whether you selected English, Türkçe or Русский.

4. Third-party services

The website may load fonts or other resources from third-party providers. Those providers may process technical information such as an IP address as part of delivering the resource. Their own privacy policies apply to that processing.

5. Data retention and requests

Burion Studio does not intentionally collect personal information through a website database at this stage. If you contact us by email, your message may be retained in the relevant email system for communication and record-keeping.

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact support@burionstudio.com.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated as the website and its services evolve. The latest version will be published on this page.
